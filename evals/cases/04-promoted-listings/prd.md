# Promoted Listings (Bump) — PRD draft

_Draft — some details still TBD with Finance and Search teams._

## Why

Sellers with lots of competition want more visibility. We'll let them pay to
push a listing higher in search for a period of time.

## How it works

- From their own listing, a seller taps **Promote**.
- They choose a package:
  - **3-day Bump** — 30 coins
  - **7-day Bump** — 60 coins
- Coins are deducted immediately on purchase. If the seller doesn't have enough
  coins they're prompted to top up.
- While promoted, the listing appears in the **top 3 positions** of search
  results for its category, rotating with other promoted listings.
- A "Promoted" label is shown on the listing card in search results.
- The seller can see time remaining on the promotion from their listing page.
- When the promotion ends, the listing returns to normal ranking and the label
  is removed.

## Eligibility

- Only **Available** listings can be promoted.
- Listings in restricted categories (see Trust & Safety list) can't be promoted.

## Reporting

- Sellers see a simple stats card: views and chats during the promotion period,
  compared to the same-length period before it.
